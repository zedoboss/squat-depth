SETUP

  1. Phone at your side. Tripod at about hip height, 2–3 m away, landscape. Not behind you: knee angle can't be read from the back.
  2. Stand sideways to the camera, the same way you'll squat under the bar.
  3. Whole body in frame, head to feet, at the top and at the bottom of the squat.
  4. Check the lock before you load up. Wait for the green LOCK pill and the thick line on your camera-side leg, then do one unloaded rep and listen for the ding.
  5. Phone speaker, volume up. No Bluetooth: it delays the ding by 150–300 ms.
