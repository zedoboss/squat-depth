SETUP

  1. Phone at your side, upright. Tripod at about hip height, 2–3 m away. Upright (portrait) fits a standing person; sideways only works if the phone is far enough back that your head stays in the picture.
  2. Stand sideways to the camera, the same way you'll squat under the bar. Not facing it, not with your back to it.
  3. Head to feet in the picture, standing and at the bottom. If your head is cut off when you stand, the app can't see you standing and the ding comes late or not at all.
  4. Wait for the double blip. Stand still where you'll squat. Two soft blips and READY mean it has you. No blip: fix the framing first. Then do one unloaded rep and listen for the ding.
  5. Phone speaker, volume up. No Bluetooth: it delays the ding by 150–300 ms.
